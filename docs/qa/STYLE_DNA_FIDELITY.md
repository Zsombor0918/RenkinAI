Score each criterion0 (fails),1 (uncertain),2 (clear) only after viewing actual PNGs:
recognizability, overall drawing coherence,48dp legibility/optical scale, alpha edges/
transparency, native-color policy and absence of source-logo borrowing. Retain
individual criteria rather than treating a total as calibrated confidence. A failed
identity or copied-reference check rejects the trial regardless of the total.

Before any optional new request, inspect actual original, overall/family choice,
human-reviewed symbol type (UNKNOWN is an explicit fallback), color policy and
matching references. Full frozen recipe/hashes remain in confirmation Details.

with fewer than three samples is provisional; it is not evidence of a pack-wide rule.

family, return to Style and review/save its matching references first. Do not mix
unrelated images to fill five slots. Do not clear data, replay uncertain plans or
