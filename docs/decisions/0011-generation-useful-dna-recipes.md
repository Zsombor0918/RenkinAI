# ADR 0011 - generation-useful DNA without a new report schema

Date: October16,2026. Status: proposed for owner review.

## Context

Overall __pack__ guidance currently derives exact repeated text and emits many generic unknown sentences. Silhouette evidence is mapped to simplification/fill instead of contour. Standard has validated family synthesis that can describe recurrence in different words. Generation needs concrete construction decisions rather than a presumed visual score or another paid request.

## Decision

Retain per-image schema1, grammar schema1, raw observations and optional families. Use existing fields for background versus standalone silhouette, geometry/contour, qualitative optical fill/padding, edge/stroke, simplification/complexity, color roles, depth, text and negative space. Brand preservation and abstraction are explicit generation constraints, not invented observed measurements. Compile supported rules with provenance/sample support, omit repeated unknown placeholders and list unsupported traits once. The separate original IMAGE1 remains identity authority; at most five chosen-style member references remain style-only.

Global rules first use at least two OBSERVED reports and60 percent recurrence. Otherwise a supported synthesized drawing rule may supply the same existing field only with at least two cited samples and60 percent pack-wide cited support. Conflicting small families cannot impose a universal rule. Such global guidance is INFERRED; raw evidence is never rewritten. Representative defaults skip explicitly flagged outliers, keep construction/type diversity and never fill a small family from unrelated members. Explicit reviewed membership checks remain unchanged.

New prompt version per-icon-grammar-v2 refines guidance within the same fields and paid-step count/caps. Readers accept v1 and v2; v1 frozen plans emit their original instructions, preserve prompt hashes/fingerprints and consumed identifiers. Results reconstructed from a journal retain the frozen plan version. Unknown versions fail closed. Existing saved specifications are not rewritten. New trial recipes are frozen and the existing current-input comparison rejects a changed recipe before submission.

## Alternatives and consequences

Adding report fields, a second overall cloud synthesis, an unverified prose classifier or a visual-quality score would expand storage/usage or imply unsupported precision. They are rejected. Recipe labels/provenance are deterministic assembly, not proof that a provider will observe or draw accurately. Keep __pack__ and family evidence inspectable. No Room21/schema19-21/backup/journal-shape migration, new endpoint or silent retry is introduced.

V2 uses the existing version string field, so rollback must retain a compatible v1/v2 reader. Older binaries reject v2 during existing validation; do not downgrade owner data or replay consumed plans. Full/share compatibility and legacy null-override transport stay covered. Actual token consumption, output completeness, Samsung rendering and visual fidelity remain unmeasured; no live/paid request is authorized.

## Raw silhouette identity boundary

The full aef0dca0 suite demonstrates that direct raw logo_silhouette mapping can carry reference identity into the recipe. Local synthesis/global recurrence therefore accept only a small closed abstract construction vocabulary from that field; unrecognized, negated or identity-appended text remains raw evidence. Supported family drawing rules remain the abstract synthesis channel. This conservative boundary may omit useful Quick contours; do not replace it with an unverified brand/semantic classifier or weaken the retained identity assertion.
